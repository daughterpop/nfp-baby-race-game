🌸 HOW TO PLAY – NFP BABY CARD GAME

📋 GAME GOAL

Couples compete over your chosen fertile years (5–25). 12 cycles per year. Whoever has the MOST babies at the end wins!

🃏 THE 28-CARD DECK

Cards represent real NFP observations (mucus, temperature, etc.):

🌵
Infertile (14 cards)
10% chance of baby
🌱
Low fertility (6 cards)
25% chance of baby
🌸
Medium fertility (5 cards)
45% chance of baby
🥚
Peak fertility (3 cards)
70% chance of baby

🤰 PREGNANCY & TIMING

  • Only couples who are currently TRYING draw a card each cycle
  • If you conceive → you are pregnant for the next 9 cycles (realistic ~9 months)
  • During those 9 cycles you skip drawing while other couples keep trying
  • After 9 cycles the baby is born and you can start trying again
  • Twins (4% chance) or triplets (0.5% chance) possible on any conception roll!

🎲 HOW EACH CYCLE WORKS

  1. Shuffle the 28-card deck
  2. Each TRYING couple draws ONE unique card
  3. Flip → roll for conception that cycle
  4. Advance to next cycle → births happen automatically if due
  5. Repeat for all 12×chosen-years cycles
Real NFP note: This simulates couples timing intercourse during the fertile window each cycle. Pregnancy pauses trying for ~9 months — exactly like real life!
